Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

SAGE is a student productivity workspace that helps people organize tasks, schedules, goals, reminders, and learning activities. This policy explains what information SAGE uses and the choices available to you.

Information you provide

You may provide profile information, academic details, tasks, goals, schedule items, preferences, reminders, and selected coding platforms. SAGE uses this information to organize your workspace and recommend relevant next actions.

Google account information

When you choose Google sign-in, SAGE receives your verified name, email address, profile image, and a Google account identifier. The Google credential is verified before an application session is created.

Google Calendar data

Google Calendar access is optional. If you connect it, SAGE can read calendar events and create, update, or remove events that you synchronize through SAGE. Calendar access is used only to provide the calendar synchronization features you request.

How information is used

  • Provide and personalize your student workspace.
  • Synchronize calendar events when you request it.
  • Generate contextual planning suggestions from information in your workspace.
  • Maintain security, prevent misuse, and troubleshoot service availability.

Sharing and advertising

SAGE does not sell personal information or Google user data. Google data is not used for advertising. Information is shared only with service providers required to operate requested features or when required by law.

Tokens and security

Google access tokens are kept in browser memory for the active session and are not placed in frontend source code. Authentication sessions use secure, HTTP-only cookies. No Google client secret is stored in the browser.

Retention and deletion

Information is retained only as needed to operate SAGE and provide the features you use. You can disconnect Google Calendar to revoke the active connection. You may also request export or deletion from Privacy & Data settings.

Your choices

You can use SAGE without connecting Google Calendar, disconnect calendar access, remove synchronized events, sign out, or revoke SAGE from your Google Account’s third-party connections page.

Children and educational use

SAGE is intended for students who are permitted to use online account services in their region. Schools and guardians remain responsible for any additional consent requirements that apply to their students.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as SAGE changes. Material updates will be reflected on this page with a revised update date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact SAGE through the support email displayed on the Google authorization screen.
